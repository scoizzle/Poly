### Issue F2 -- Severity: suggestion

- **File**: `Poly.Tests/TestHelpers/ValidDomain.cs:20` (and `:26`)

- **Suggestion**: drop the `Where` and let `ExtensionCatalog.Core.Resolve(id)` throw on an unknown id.
- **Status**: open.

### Issue F3 -- Severity: nit
