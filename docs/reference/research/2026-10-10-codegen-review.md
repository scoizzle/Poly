## Notes

- The typed surface (private ctor + static `Create`, stage enums, stage-guard checks, policy methods, for-invoke fan-out, `when` subscription wiring) is faithful, warning-free, and mostly idiomatic; the real problems concentrate in (a) the K0 dictionary create path, (b) the two always-success / always-fail stubs (D1/D2), and (c) the DslCompiler host-file generator (D3–D6, D16, D17), which is a separate, less-polished emitter.
- `EnsureUnique` (D1) is the headline bug: every `unique` in the DSL is a lie in shipped code and the call site still fires, misleading a reader into thinking it is enforced.
- The `when all` handler `WhenAllWaitOfferCancelled(previousStage)` in university/Section.cs.golden correctly uses `previousStage`, so D12 applies only to the non-`all` notifiers — the parameter is not uniformly dead.
- D3's duplicate endpoints trace to stage-scoped actions being enumerated once per declaring stage; D4's `FirstOrDefault` and D6's enum `HasColumnType` are independent correctness gaps in the same generator worth fixing together.
